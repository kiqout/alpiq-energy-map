# Photo credits and licenses

All cards use real photographs from Wikimedia Commons or the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation. The **Production by technology** section and **Environmental impact comparison** now use separate image sets. The six Environmental impact comparison cards no longer reuse any image from the production cards.

The technology photographs can be downloaded into `public/media/` by running `npm run media:download`. Until then, those cards fall back to the public Wikimedia image URLs and require an internet connection. Environmental photographs remain stored in `public/media/`.
